Frequently Asked Questions

When are the annual homeowner assessments due?

The annual homeowner assessments are due by April 15th of each year, after which a late fee of $30.00 will be applied. There is a $10.00 discount for any resident who pays their dues prior to January 31st of each year. If you have not received your invoice for HOA fees, please email our bookkeeper at montclairbookkeeper@gmail.com.

Where do I send the homeowner dues payment?

You can mail a check or pay your HOA dues online. Mail payments to Montclair Association, Inc., P.O. Box 204015, Augusta, GA 30917. If you have not received your invoice for HOA fees, please email montclairbookkeeper@gmail.com.

Who do I call to report a streetlight that is burned out?

Call Georgia Power at 1-888-891-0938 or Augusta 311 to report any streetlights out. Be prepared to give them an address or intersection.

Can we swim at the pool if we do not live in Montclair?

Guests of members are welcome at the pool but must be accompanied by a pool member and pay any guest fees that apply. Pool memberships are offered to Montclair residents and non-residents. You can fill out a membership form at the pool or pay for your membership online. For more information, see the Pool page or email montclairpoolaug@gmail.com.

Are the tennis courts available free to residents?

No. The tennis and pickleball courts require membership. For current resident and non-resident rates, see the Pickleball Courts page or email arheins@gmail.com.

Is the pavilion near the pool available for rent?

Yes! The pavilion is free for Montclair residents but must be reserved by emailing montclairhoa17@gmail.com or montclairpavilion@gmail.com. The pavilion can be rented by non-residents for a small fee. See the Forms page for the "Pavilion Rental Agreement".

I want to make changes to the outside of my house. Do I need approval?

Possibly. The covenants regulate certain architectural elements, so it is best to get approval by filling out the Architectural Modification Request Form or emailing montclairhoa17@gmail.com.

How do I get a copy of the By-Laws and Covenants?

Download a copy from the Covenants & By-Laws page.

To whom do I report an issue at one of the parks?

To report any issue with a common area, park, the tennis courts, or the pool, contact montclairhoa17@gmail.com or a board member. Any emergency or criminal activity should be reported to the Richmond County Sheriff’s Office at (706) 821-1080.

Who do I call to report homeless or vagrant activity?

The Richmond County Marshal’s Office at (706) 821-2368.

Who do I call if I see suspicious activity?

The Richmond County Sheriff’s Office at (706) 821-1080.

What should I report to the City of Augusta?

These requests should be made to Augusta Cares 311 at (706) 821-2300, or through the MyAugusta 311 app (SeeClickFix for iPhone and Android):

  • Repairing a sidewalk or replacing a missing street sign
  • Resurfacing or patching a road
  • Trash pickup, or trash that was missed
  • Complaints about city ordinance violations
  • Mosquito treatment for your yard
