Pool

The Montclair pool is a 25-yard, L-shaped pool complete with two diving boards.

The secured, fenced area is surrounded on three sides by trees that provide privacy, with ample space for catching rays, picnicking, or just relaxing by the pool. Montclair Pool is “Swim at Your Own Risk,” which allows the pool to be available from May until early October (weather permitting). Lifeguards are on duty during peak times starting Memorial Day and before school starts back in August. Pool memberships are available to Montclair residents and non-residents.

All pool access is monitored via security system. All key code entry and guest fees are enforced.
