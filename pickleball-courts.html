Pickleball Courts

Montclair’s Crane Ferry Park has eight pickleball courts.

Our members put their best paddles forward and have created a unique community that fosters good games, fun, friends, and a welcoming atmosphere to all who join.

Pickleball is a social, easy-to-learn sport that is sweeping the nation in popularity. Join the fun! Learn to Play classes are available.
