Welcome to Montclair

One of Augusta, Georgia’s most desired neighborhoods since its development in 1964, Montclair prides itself on a long tradition of its warm family atmosphere and friendly neighbors.

Located in West Richmond County, a short 3 miles west of the famous golf course, you will find well-built homes on spacious lots with well-established trees and flora. With over 530 homes, Montclair features wide streets, sidewalks, streetlights, green space, and two neighborhood parks. Our parks feature a large members’ swimming pool and newly resurfaced tennis and pickleball courts.
